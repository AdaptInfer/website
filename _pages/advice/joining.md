Thanks for your interest in the Adaptive Inference Lab. This page covers research positions for people who are already at UW-Madison, postdocs, and remote collaborators.
If you are applying to a PhD program, see [Advice for Prospective Students](./prospective_students) instead. PhD admissions go through the department, not through me.

**Current status:** I have very limited capacity right now. If you can drive a research project mostly on your own, I'm happy to give some advising along the way. If you're hoping to learn a new area from scratch, this isn't a good time to apply. I try to reply to every application.

## Who can apply

- **Research scientists and postdocs**
- **Undergraduate researchers** who are already enrolled at UW-Madison
- **Master's students** who are already enrolled at UW-Madison
- **PhD students** who are already enrolled at UW-Madison
- **Remote collaborators**

## How to apply

1. Fill out the [application form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSctsfvSMoNk9YnCYSGWTYE2he-dR3lpD9QOmQaRtCvwH3X7Hg/viewform).
2. Email your CV to lengerich@wisc.edu.

If you are at UW-Madison, please use your wisc.edu address.

## Making your application stand out

The form asks short questions, mostly 1–2 sentences each. Short answers are hard to write well, so they tell me a lot.

- **Pick specific projects.** Read [our current projects](/projects) and choose the ones that genuinely fit you. Saying why a project fits you is more useful than checking every box.
- **Be concrete about your goals.** "Learn ML" is hard to act on. "Build a model of X to answer Y, and apply to PhD programs in Z" tells me how we could work together.
- **Answer the strengths and weaknesses questions honestly.** I'm looking for self-awareness, not a perfect candidate.
- **For publications, say what you did.** Underline your name and describe your role in a few words.

## What to expect

Research takes sustained time. Projects go best when students can commit regular hours across a full semester or more.
Plan on at least **10 hours a week** for a full semester.

Student positions are for research credit or as a volunteer; we generally can't offer pay.

See [People](/people) for current members and alumni.
